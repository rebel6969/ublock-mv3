Every list refreshes in the browser — no rebuild. “Update all lists” refetches and recompiles them. Smaller lists (full) are replaced outright. Larger ones (patched) ship as a fixed baseline that Chrome will not let an extension rewrite, so only the differences are applied: new rules are added and rules dropped upstream are switched off. A rebuild is only ever needed to reset that baseline once the accumulated differences fill their reserved space — the bar below turns amber well before then.
